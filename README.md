# DevList.app support

Questions, problems with a listing, and privacy or account requests for
[DevList.app](https://devlist.app) go here: [open an issue](https://github.com/eas4ai/devlist-support/issues/new).

Issues are public. Do not post your email address, password or other
personal details. For a request about your account, give the name on your
account and say what you want; we reply on the issue.
